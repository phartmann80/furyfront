# Operators

Launch roster: **8**. Season 1: **+4**. V0.1 playable: **Vex (Assault) only**.

Each operator is a **silhouette first**, ability second. Abilities are **tactical, not hero ultimates**. Cooldowns are long enough that gunplay remains the skill expression.

## Shared rules

- Passive always on.
- Active: 1 button (D-pad / `Q` / ability). No second ultimate.
- Ranked: all operators allowed. No paid operators with extra HP.
- Customization: 4 outfit slots (head, torso, legs, gear), 2 charm slots, 1 finisher.
- Unlock: account level or battle pass. **Stats do not scale with cosmetics.**

**Level-based operator progression (account, not prestige wipe):**

| Operator level | Unlock |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Passive + default skin |
| 5 | Active ability (if gated) — launch ops have active at 1 |
| 10 | Recolor |
| 20 | Legendary outfit piece |
| 30 | Finisher |
| 40 | Reactive skin (seasonal) |

Operator XP is match XP tagged to the selected operator (100% of match XP).

## Launch roster

### Vex — Assault
- **Silhouette:** Compact, short undercut, light plate, SMG-ready stance. Smallest torso block.
- **Passive — Bloodrush:** +8% tactical sprint duration; reload 10% faster below 50 HP.
- **Active — Stim Dash (28 s):** 6 m burst, 15 HP over 2 s, 0.4 s gun ready delay.
- **Voice:** Sharp, impatient.  
- **Unlock:** Level 1 default.

### Bishop — Tactician
- **Silhouette:** Radio backpack, beret, rectangular vest, antenna line.
- **Passive — Chain of Command:** Squad sees your ping 20% longer; +10% objective score.
- **Active — Pulse Sweep (45 s):** 1.2 s local UAV, 18 m, enemies on minimap 4 s (walls).
- **Unlock:** Level 8.

### Nyx — Stealth
- **Silhouette:** Hood, wrapped lower face, broken outline (cape shard). Low head profile.
- **Passive — Soft Step:** Footstep loudness −35% while crouched; no red name until 12 m.
- **Active — Veil (40 s):** 3.5 s crouch-sprint with suppressed silhouette (not invisible). Firing breaks.
- **Unlock:** Level 12.

### Golem — Heavy
- **Silhouette:** Oversized pauldrons, thick legs, widest operator by far.
- **Passive — Plated:** 15 absorb armor (regens out of combat 8 s). Movement −6%.
- **Active — Riot Wall (50 s):** Deploy cover 2.2 × 1.4 m, 250 HP, 20 s.
- **Unlock:** Level 15.

### Wraith — Recon
- **Silhouette:** Slim long coat, rangefinder on helmet, triangular hood.
- **Passive — Tracker:** Enemies you damage stay marked 1.5 s for you only.
- **Active — Heartbeat (35 s):** 2.5 s cone 24 m, pings hostiles not using Nyx Veil.
- **Unlock:** Level 18.

### Rook — Support
- **Silhouette:** Square pack, visible plate carrier, medic slash on sleeve.
- **Passive — Splints:** Revive (Arena/Horde) 25% faster; nearby allies regenerate +2 HP/s after 4 s idle.
- **Active — Plate Toss (40 s):** Throw 25 absorb to ally (or self), 20 s duration.
- **Unlock:** Level 22.

### Hex — Disruptor
- **Silhouette:** Angular helmet, cable webbing, asymmetric arm cannon-bracer (non-weapon).
- **Passive — Grounded:** EMP duration on you −30%.
- **Active — Blackout (55 s):** 8 m EMP, 3 s electronics (minimap, streaks, heartbeats, optics glint).
- **Unlock:** Level 28.

### Sable — Marksman
- **Silhouette:** Long cape-break at calf, wide-brim patrol cap, rifle-forward lean.
- **Passive — Cold Bore:** First shot after 1.2 s ADS idle: +10% range, no glint for that shot.
- **Active — Hold (30 s):** 2.8 s sway −80%, flinch −50%, breath overlay.
- **Unlock:** Level 35.

## Ability UX

- HUD: diamond bottom-left, cooldown sweep.
- Telegraph: all actives have 0.15 s deploy so they can be heard.
- Counterplay: Hex EMP, smoke vs Wraith, fire vs Nyx Veil, explosives vs Golem wall.

## Customization economy

Default outfits free. Store/battle pass pieces. **Hitboxes do not change** with outfits (use hidden standard capsules; cloth is render-only). Legendary capes use simplified collision-ignore.

Distinct silhouette test: 40 m, 0.5 s glance, 80% identification in playtest or the outfit is rejected.
